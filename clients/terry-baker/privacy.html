Privacy Policy

Effective November 27, 2026

Terry Baker ("I," "me," or "my") is an independent hairstylist in Parrish, Florida. This policy explains what information I collect when you call or text to book an appointment, how I use it, and the choices you have.

Information I collect

  • Booking details. When you call or text my booking number, I collect your name, phone number, the service you want, and your appointment date and time.
  • Consent records. I record whether you agreed to appointment calls and to appointment text messages, and when you agreed.
  • Calls. My booking line is answered by a virtual assistant. Calls may be recorded and summarized so I can confirm your booking and improve the service. You are told this at the start of each call.

How I use it

  • To book, confirm, and manage your appointments.
  • To send appointment confirmations and reminders, and to reply to your questions, if you agreed to receive them.
  • To keep basic records of my appointments.

Text messaging (SMS)

If you opt in, you will receive text messages from Terry Baker about your appointments: confirmations, reminders, and replies to your questions. I do not send marketing or promotional texts. Message frequency varies, usually a few messages per appointment. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out and HELP for help. See my Terms and Conditions for full messaging terms.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the categories above exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Sharing

I do not sell your personal information. My booking system is set up and managed for me by S Jackson Services, and it uses phone, messaging, calendar, and virtual assistant providers to work. They handle your information only to run my booking service. I may also disclose information when required by law.

Retention and security

I keep booking records only as long as needed to serve you and keep basic business records, then delete them. Reasonable steps are taken to protect your information, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any text message to stop receiving texts.
  • Call or text 941-401-4990 to ask what information I hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted.

Children

My booking line is meant for adults. If you are booking for a child, a parent or guardian should make the booking.

Changes

If I update this policy, I will post the new version here with a new effective date.
