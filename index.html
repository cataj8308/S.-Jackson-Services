What We Do

Our Services

Four things, done well. If you're not sure which one you need, just give us a call and we'll talk it through in plain language.

Welding

Repairs and fabrication in steel and aluminum. Brackets, supports, equipment frames, kitchen and shop fixtures, and the odd job that nobody else wants to touch.

Good forRestaurants, shops, contractors and fabricators

Inspection

A careful set of eyes on your equipment, welds, structures, or systems. You get a straight answer about what's safe, what's wearing out, and what can wait.

Good forContractors, welders and inspection companies

Auditing

A thorough review of your equipment, processes, or safety practices, with a written report you can actually read. No jargon, just what's working and what needs attention.

Good forLocal businesses, facilities and contracting firms

Technology Automation

We set up the tools that save you time: scheduling, reminders, digital records, and simple systems that run on their own so you don't have to chase paperwork.

Good forBusy owners, salons, restaurants and trade businesses

About Us

A Father & Daughter Team

S Jackson Services is a family business. Steve brings decades of hands-on trade experience in welding, inspection, and auditing. His daughter Catherine handles the technology side, keeping everything organized and helping other businesses do the same.

That mix means you get old-school workmanship with new-school convenience: clear quotes, on-time appointments, and reports you can find later when you need them.

Not sure who to call? Either one of us is glad to point you in the right direction.
